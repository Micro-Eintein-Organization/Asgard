# ESCO classification

> This service uses the ESCO classification of the European Commission.

Source release for the Asgard integration is ESCO v1.2.1, English classification CSVs, published by the European Commission's Directorate-General for Employment, Social Affairs and Inclusion. The offline importer pins the exact archive inspected for Asgard. [Download ESCO](https://esco.ec.europa.eu/en/use-esco/download)

The Commission permits free reuse, reproduction, and mapping for commercial and noncommercial purposes under its specific ESCO conditions. Publish the acknowledgement above where the service uses ESCO. Clearly identify adaptations and carry the notice with any distributed extract. [ESCO download conditions](https://esco.ec.europa.eu/en/use-esco/download/privacy-statement) · [Commission Decision 2011/833/EU](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32011D0833)

Asgard's staging index groups records by source URI, preserves source relationship labels, and represents omitted trailing hierarchy fields as null in its normalized view. Any public subset, career crosswalk, or edited description must identify Asgard's changes alongside the source version.

Third-party rights review is pending for ISCO-08 and material identified in ESCO's skills copyright notice. Keep unresolved fields and records out of public extracts and runtime views.
